## Part F — Privacy page draft

### Join the campaign list

This is a student concept campaign created for a marketing class. It is not an official Duolingo communication and is not affiliated with Duolingo.

**Who we are.** This list is managed by **[student/team name]**, a student project at LEAD College of Management, Palakkad. Contact: **[project email]**.

**What we collect.** We ask for your email address, and you may optionally provide your first name. We record your separate choices about campaign emails and personalised offers. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

**Why we collect it.** We use your email to send campaign updates only if you opt in. If you separately opt in to personalisation, we use only preferences you choose to share to tailor those updates. You can leave either choice unticked.

**How long we keep it.** We delete your information when you withdraw consent or 12 months after the campaign ends, whichever comes first, unless a law requires longer retention.

**Who receives it.** We do not sell your information or share it with campaign partners. The form and response sheet are hosted using Google Forms/Google Drive, which may process the information as the service provider under its terms.

**Your choices and rights.** You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, withdraw either consent, or raise a concern by contacting **[project email]**. Withdrawing is as easy as emailing us; it will not affect earlier processing. You can also contact the applicable privacy authority if your concern is not resolved.

**Language.** This notice is available in English. **[Add Tamil translation/link if the form targets Tamil-speaking participants.]**

### Google Form fields

1. Email — required; collect manually in a short-answer field. Do not enable automatic account email collection unless needed.
2. First name — optional.
3. Checkboxes, both unticked and independently optional:
   - Email me campaign updates.
   - Use my answers to personalise offers.
4. Required checkbox with one option: “I confirm that I am 18 or older.”
5. Form description: “You can withdraw at any time by emailing [project email]. See our privacy notice: [privacy page link].”

Use separate consent options, no pre-ticked boxes, no forced marketing consent, no false urgency, and an equally easy withdrawal path. The privacy notice should identify the real student/team contact before publishing. For the legal text, verify against the [official DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023-1.pdf).
