### Objectives and strategy

- Proposed objective: weekly retained learners +15% against the first 14-day baseline; D30 retention +10%; paid conversion +0.5 percentage point; branded-search impression share at least 85% weekly. These are proposals, not Duolingo forecasts.
- Target consented first-party behavioural groups: new learners (days 0–7), habit-forming learners, active paid learners, and at-risk learners flagged by declining sessions, longer time since login, payment failure, or rising support contacts. Risk indicators are from D5 practice and require validation on real data.
- Positioning: Duolingo offers free, playful short-session language practice; DoomCopy can copy ads or price but not instantly recreate the established product experience and community.
- Growth loop: useful lessons → progress → optional sharing/referrals → new learners. Streak-rescue prompts are opt-in, frequency-capped, and easy to turn off.

Creator disclosure, capped brand search, sample-size confirmation, and an AI FAQ guardrail are specified in the worksheet.

### Action and control

- Days 1–30 ($80k): verify event definitions and baselines; approve privacy/retention; launch capped brand defense and pilot welcome / 7-day inactivity messages.
- Days 31–70 ($90k): onboarding A/B test; randomized 10% lifecycle holdout; disclosed creator partnerships; test capped at-risk and failed-payment flows.
- Days 71–100 ($80k): scale only positive incremental contribution; pause wasteful search; report retention/conversion/lift; audit privacy and vendors.
- Attribution choice: position-based, 40% first touch, 20% middle touches, 40% last touch. Compare against last click; use holdout for causal lift.
- D5 practice churn features: days since last login, sessions in 30 days, payment failures, support tickets, tenure, and discount join flag. The saved plan reports logistic regression AUC .891 vs tree .648, with explicit practice-data caveat.
- Incrementality test: randomized 10% holdout of eligible opted-in new/reactivated learners, no promotional rescue message for 30 days; compare retention, paid conversion, and contribution per eligible learner.
- Privacy: purpose-specific consent; data minimisation; retention limits; access, deletion, and withdrawal; vendor review; breach response; GDPR for EU and DPDP requirements for India before launch.
- Risks named: ad/brand-bid copying (capped defence and impression-share monitoring); last-click over-credit (randomized holdout); privacy/AI errors (approved content, human fallback, minimisation, audits).
