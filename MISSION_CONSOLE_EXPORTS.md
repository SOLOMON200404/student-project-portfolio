# Mission Console export: Days 1–6

This index records the saved Mission Console activity results and links the actual local workbooks, datasets, and project files in this private repository. The day-specific `Mission-Console-Worksheet.md` files contain the worksheet values read from the console. Nothing in this export was sealed or submitted.

All activity scores/statuses are in [`Mission-Console-Activity-Results.csv`](Mission-Console-Activity-Results.csv). All URLs found in the saved lab worksheets are indexed in [`Mission-Console-Evidence-URLs.csv`](Mission-Console-Evidence-URLs.csv). The list distinguishes private evidence links, instructional resources, and legacy LinguaLeap URLs. One-time verification-style evidence URLs are intentionally redacted.

## Source and data boundaries

- Mission Console: https://claude.ai/artifact/C28zArhpJdurgmWCQ3LWR3
- D1–D5 datasets are fictional class-practice data, not Duolingo analytics.
- Day 2 figures are from the Google Merchandise Store GA4 demo, not Duolingo.
- The Day 1 M1-2 sheet is a legacy LinguaLeap worksheet, despite the console's Duolingo Earth label. It is preserved as recorded and clearly marked; it must not be represented as Duolingo research.
- Private evidence URLs that resemble one-time tokens are redacted in the CSV export.
- Sheets that remain in Google Sheets are linked in the day-specific notes; this repo now also contains the filled values that were visible in Mission Console.
